LANGUAGE & LIFE

Discover Turkish culture

Everyday expressions and cultural habits in Turkish.

☕

Çay culture

Tea is shared throughout the day with friends and guests. “Bir çay alabilir miyim?” means “Could I have a tea?”

🍽️

Afiyet olsun

Said before or after eating or drinking: “Enjoy your meal / May it bring health.”

🤝

Kolay gelsin

Said to someone working: “May your work go easily.”
