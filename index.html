LANGUAGE & LIFE

Discover Turkish culture

Language makes more sense when you meet the culture behind it.

☕

Çay culture

Tea is often shared with friends, family and guests. “Bir çay alabilir miyim?” means “Could I have a tea?”

🍽️

Afiyet olsun

A warm phrase said before or after someone eats. It is similar to “Enjoy your meal.”

🤝

Kolay gelsin

A kind phrase said to someone who is working: “May your work go easily.”
