LANGUAGE & LIFE

Discover Turkish culture

Everyday expressions used across Turkish social life.

☕

Çay culture

Tea is central to hospitality and daily breaks. “Bir çay alabilir miyim?” means “Could I have a tea?”

🍽️

Afiyet olsun

Said before or after eating or drinking: “Enjoy your meal / May it bring health.”

🤝

Kolay gelsin

Said to someone working: “May your work go easily.”
